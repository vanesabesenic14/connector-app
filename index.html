Connector
PrivacyTerms

Your accounts, connected to your own assistant

Connector is a small personal app that runs on your own computer. It lets you connect accounts you own, such as TikTok, YouTube and Instagram, and then use them from an AI assistant: read your profile stats, list your videos, and upload videos you made.

How it works

  1. You run Connector on your computer. It is not a public service and has no user accounts.
  2. You sign in to each platform with that platform's own login page and choose what to allow.
  3. Your sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on your computer only. Nothing is sent to us.
  4. You can disconnect any account at any time, inside Connector or in the platform's own settings.

What it uses from TikTok

  • Login Kit: to sign in with your TikTok account.
  • Display API (user.info.basic, user.info.profile, user.info.stats, video.list): to show your own profile, follower counts and videos.
  • Content Posting API (video.upload, video.publish): to upload videos you created to your own account, as drafts or posts you approve.

Connector never posts without the account owner's action, never reads other people's private data, and never sells or shares data.

Connector is a personal, non-commercial project. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
