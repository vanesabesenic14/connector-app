Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Connector is a personal application that runs on the user's own computer. This policy explains what data it handles when you connect TikTok, YouTube (Google), Instagram or Facebook (Meta) accounts.

Who we are

Connector is a personal, non-commercial project. It has no servers that receive your data. Contact: GitHub issues for this project.

What data Connector accesses

Only data from accounts you connect yourself, and only the permissions you approve on the platform's own sign-in page:

  • TikTok: your basic profile (display name, avatar, username, bio), profile statistics (followers, following, likes, video count), the list of your public videos with their statistics, and the ability to upload or publish videos you choose.
  • YouTube / Google: your channel, videos, playlists, comments, captions and analytics, and the ability to upload and manage your own content.
  • Instagram / Facebook: your professional Instagram account and the Facebook Pages you manage: profile, media, comments, messages and insights, and the ability to publish content you choose.

How data is used

  • To show you your own account information and to carry out actions you request, such as uploading a video.
  • Data is not used for advertising, profiling or any purpose other than the action you asked for.
  • Data is never sold, rented or shared with third parties.

Where data is stored

Sign-in tokens and app credentials are stored encrypted on your own computer. Account data fetched from a platform is shown to you and not stored, except where you save it yourself. Connector does not run any server that collects, receives or keeps your data.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Click Disconnect for a platform in Connector to delete its stored tokens immediately.
  • You can also remove Connector's access in each platform's settings (TikTok: Settings → Security → Manage app permissions; Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions; Meta: Settings → Apps and websites).
  • Deleting Connector's data folder from your computer removes everything it stored.

Data deletion requests

Because Connector stores nothing on any server, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. To delete data held on your computer, disconnect the account in Connector or delete Connector's data folder. For help, open a GitHub issue.

Children

Connector is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required by the connected platform).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
